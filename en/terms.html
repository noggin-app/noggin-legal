The service

Noggin is an app where parents measure their baby's head shape with a 3D scan, photos, or a tape measure and see it as numbers and pictures. You need an iPhone to use it, and there is no sign-up.

Not a diagnosis

Noggin's results are reference measurements. They do not replace a doctor's diagnosis or treatment, and Noggin is not a medical device. If a result worries you, or you wonder about your baby's head shape or growth, please talk to a doctor such as a pediatrician or a rehabilitation medicine doctor. Please don't put off treatment or a clinic visit based on a result alone.

Who uses it

This app is for a baby's parent or guardian to use directly. Your baby's details and measurements are calculated from what you enter and photograph. The more accurate the photos and tape measure values are, the more accurate the result is. Your baby's safety comes first when you take pictures. Please measure only when your baby is comfortable and in a safe position.

Pass

You need a pass to see results. The Day Pass is a one-time purchase that opens the result of the measurement you paid for and the results of every measurement you start within 24 hours of the purchase (by the App Store purchase time). Tape measure, photo, and 3D measurements you add later to an opened measurement are opened too. If you measure again after 24 hours, you pay again. The Monthly Pass is a subscription that is charged automatically every month and opens every result you measure during that period. The price is shown on the purchase screen.

Payment and cancelling

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account. The Monthly Pass renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the next payment. You can cancel or change it in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, or in the app under Settings > Pass > Manage subscription. If you cancel, you can keep using it until the end of the period you already paid for.

Withdrawal and refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Under Apple's refund policy, you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com .

Once a pass opens a result, delivery of digital content has begun, so withdrawal may be limited under Article 17(2) of the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. We tell you this on the purchase screen beforehand.

Results opened with a refunded purchase lock again.

Purchases by minors

If a minor made a purchase without the consent of a legal guardian, the minor or the guardian can cancel it. Cancel by requesting a refund from Apple.

Results you already opened

Results opened with a pass stay viewable after the pass ends. If you delete the app or delete your records, the results are deleted too.

Records and deleting

Your baby's details, photos, 3D models, and measurements are stored only on this phone. You can delete everything at any time with Settings > Delete records, and it cannot be undone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Changes to the service and terms

The app's features and prices may change. We tell you about important changes in the app. A pass you already bought keeps what was promised when you bought it.

When we change these terms, we post a notice in the app and on this page 7 days before they take effect (30 days before for changes that are unfavorable to users).
