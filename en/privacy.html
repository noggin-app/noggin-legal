Noggin

Privacy Policy

At a glance

Noggin has no servers of ours. Your baby's details and measurement records are stored only on this phone and are not sent to us. The only thing that leaves the phone is the information needed to confirm a purchase. There are no ads and no tracking, and we put no analytics tools (SDKs) in the app. Your purchase record appears in RevenueCat's sales statistics.

Purpose of use

We use it only to calculate your baby's head measurements and show records and changes, and to confirm your pass (purchase). We don't use it for anything else.

What we handle

Your baby's name (or nickname), date of birth, and sex; the due date (if born early); head photos; 3D head models; measurements and results; and reminder settings. This information is processed only on this phone, and the operator cannot receive or see it.

Head measurements can relate to a baby's health, so we handle them with the most care. They are not sent off the phone.

Where it is stored

All records are stored in the app's own folder. While the phone is locked, they are protected from being read by device encryption. The original video of a 3D scan is deleted once the calculation is done, and only the 3D head model is kept. Photos are not saved to your photo library.

What leaves the phone

Apple and RevenueCat process payments. Apple keeps the record of purchases made with your Apple ID (under Apple's privacy policy). To confirm your pass, RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID attached to this installation, Apple's purchase record (receipt), the time of last access, and the device type and operating system. Your baby's name, photos, measurements, and your email are not sent.

We don't give or sell this information to anyone else.

Processing outsourcing and transfer abroad

Recipient: RevenueCat, Inc. (United States, privacy contact compliance@revenuecat.com)

What is transferred: the anonymous ID, purchase record, access time, and device information listed under 'What leaves the phone'

When and how: sent over an encrypted internet connection each time the app checks your pass status.

Purpose: payment and pass confirmation

Retention period: kept under RevenueCat's retention policy, only as long as needed for legal obligations and dispute resolution. If you want it deleted, tell us at the contact email below. We ask RevenueCat to delete it.

Basis: this is outsourced processing that is essential to providing the pass, so we state it in this policy and transfer it (Article 28-8(1)(iii) of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act).

If you refuse: don't use payment and pass features, or delete the app. If you refuse, you cannot use a pass to open results.

Retention and deletion

Records on the phone are kept until you delete them or delete the app. When you delete them, they are destroyed from this phone right then and cannot be undone.

iPhone backups

If iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) is on, this app's records go into that backup too. The backup is managed within your own Apple account and device, and we cannot see it. When you move to a new iPhone, your records return from the backup. If you don't want them in the backup, turn that item off in iPhone Settings.

Permissions we use

Camera: used only to photograph and 3D-scan your baby's head. Notifications: used only to remind you one day before the next measurement (we ask only if you turn it on, and it is set only on this phone). When you pick photos from your library, only the photos you pick are passed to the app. We don't use location, microphone, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

Deleting

Tap Settings > Delete records and your baby's details, photos, 3D models, measurements, and reminders are all deleted from this phone. They are deleted when you delete the app, too. Your pass record stays with Apple, so you can bring it back with Restore purchases. Old records that went into a backup disappear when a new backup overwrites them, or when you delete the backup in iPhone Settings.

Children's information

Noggin is an app for parents; children do not sign up or use it themselves. For a baby under 14, the legal guardian enters the information directly, and it is used only on this phone. You can delete everything at any time with Delete records. We don't send it to anyone or sell it.

Your rights

You can view, correct, and delete your baby's information in the app at any time. If you want to see, correct, delete, or stop the processing of information RevenueCat holds, please ask at the contact email below. We will tell you the result within 10 days.

How we keep it safe

We run no servers and don't send records off the phone. Records are protected by device encryption, and purchase confirmation is sent only over an encrypted connection. We put no secret keys in the app, and we run a security check before every release.

Automatic collection tools

We put no automatic collection tools in the app, such as cookies, advertising identifiers, or analytics and tracking SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes, we post a notice in the app and on this page 7 days before it takes effect. For a change that would send information off the phone, we ask for your consent first.
