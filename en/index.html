Noggin support

Noggin is an iPhone app where parents measure their baby's head shape with a 3D scan, photos or a tape measure and see it as numbers and pictures. Results are a measurement for reference, not a diagnosis.

Common questions

Where are my records stored?
Only on this iPhone. Noggin has no server.

How do I get my purchase back?
In the app, tap Settings › Pass › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the Monthly Pass?
In iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You can also open it from the app: Settings › Pass › Manage subscription.

I can't see the 3D scan.
The 3D scan works only on iPhones with LiDAR (Pro models). Other iPhones can measure with photos and a tape measure.

How do I delete everything?
In the app, tap Settings › Delete records. Everything is removed from this iPhone.
